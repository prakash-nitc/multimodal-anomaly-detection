# 08_understanding/ — what to read

Three documents.

## `01_HANDBOOK_NOV2026.md` — start here

The current handbook, in plain language, for the final model (PE-Core + calm phrases +
phrase bank). It has a one-page summary with a memory card, the story from the first idea
to the sealed UBnormal test (0.747), the two papers and the report, the final model step
by step, results and how to defend them, 26 likely questions with answers, ways to teach
it, a glossary and a self-quiz. Word version: `DA-ZVAD_Handbook_Nov2026.docx`
(`DA-ZVAD_Handbook.docx` is the October edition of the older handbook).

## `00_HANDBOOK.md` — the detailed reference for Phases 1–3

The original, longer handbook: the sentence idea, the measurement problems and their fixes,
the per-camera and UCF-Crime results, and the measurement audit. Kept as a reference. Some
claims there were later corrected; for example, UCF-Crime is above LAVAD and AnyAnomaly but
below the newest methods, not level with the best. Where the two handbooks differ, the
November edition is current.

## `03_domain_adaptation_deep_dive.md` — the six survey papers

The detailed study of the domain-adaptation surveys assigned by the supervisor:
what each paper contributes, the four types of shift, and the vocabulary a panel
may test.
